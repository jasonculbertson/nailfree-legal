How It Works

Three steps to nail freedom.

1

Identify your triggers

Learn what situations, emotions, and environments trigger your nail biting. Awareness is the foundation of change.

2

Intercept the urge

Build the new habit

Daily check-ins, streaks, and progress photos reinforce your identity as someone who doesn't bite their nails.
